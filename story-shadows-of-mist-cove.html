Shadows of Mist Cove

Returning from a four-year deep-sea engineering expedition, Clara Vance discovers her estranged ex-husband has declared her dead to seize her family's coastal estate—and locked their young son away in a remote signal tower.

Fictional story. Contains themes of family betrayal, child isolation, and legal drama.

Chapter 1: The Return to Mist Cove

Clara Vance adjusted the strap of her heavy canvas sea-bag against her shoulder as the diesel engines of the coastal ferry throttled down into the damp silence of Mist Cove. For four long years, her life had been measured in fathoms, steel cables, and high-pressure salvage operations off the stormy Aleutian Trench. The sub-sea telecommunications expedition had operated under strict federal non-disclosure protocols and mandatory radio blackouts across extended shifts in frozen waters. She had accepted the grueling assignment for one singular purpose: to secure enough specialized engineering capital to clear the debt on her family’s historic maritime boathouse and guarantee a permanent future for her nine-year-old son, Leo.

In the pocket of her heavy wool coat rested a small wooden sea otter Leo had carved for her from soft cedar driftwood the morning she left. Whenever North Pacific gales battered the hull of her research vessel, she would trace the smooth grain of the cedar, repeating the pledge she had made on the timber dock: I will always come back to the cove. She had survived pitch-black trench dives, equipment failures, and endless nights of isolation anchored entirely by that single memory of her son’s bright, observant eyes.
