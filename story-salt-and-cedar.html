FICTIONAL STORY · A-436

Salt and Cedar

When structural engineer Evelyn Vance returns to Cape Solitude to audit a historic pier restoration, she confronts her former mentor who framed her for a disastrous collapse years ago. Armed with physical evidence recovered from flooded sea vaults, she fights to reclaim her reputation and bring a systemic fraud to light.

Fictional story. Contains depictions of workplace sabotage, structural collapse hazards, and intense professional confrontation.

Chapter 1: The Weight of Salt and Cedar

Five years after the disaster at Sentinel Rock Lighthouse, Evelyn Vance stepped back onto the mist-shrouded boardwalk of Cape Solitude. The smell of salt spray and damp Pacific red cedar was exactly as she remembered, heavy with the sharp tang of low tide. She adjusted her leather satchel, feeling the weight of the technical blueprints and structural density logs resting against her ribs. She was no longer the novice associate who had fled the town in disgrace; she was an independent inspector hired by the regional harbor authority to conduct a mandatory audit on the historic North Pier restoration.

Inside the timbered town hall, rain rattled against the high transom windows. Evelyn stood at the oak podium before the municipal council, her voice steady as she displayed high-resolution cross-section scans of the sea wall. "The loads calculated for the lower framing exceed safety thresholds by forty percent," she said, tapping the acoustic resonance telemetry on the projection screen. "The heartwood cedar beams specified in the restoration contract have been substituted with sub-standard yellow pine treated to mimic aged timber."
