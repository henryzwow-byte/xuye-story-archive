The Harbor Clockmaker's Secret

When a naval architect's daughter falls silent after a lighthouse collapse, an orphaned girl with a master clockmaker's brass key arrives to unlock the truth behind the tragedy.

Fictional story. Contains themes of grief, psychological trauma, and family secrets.

Chapter 1: The Echo of Fog and Iron

Julian Miller did not open his coastal estate to strangers out of generous warmth. He kept the tall iron gates of Oakhaven Manor locked against the Pacific gales because grief had slowly eroded his world down to a single high-ceilinged observatory overlooking the Oregon cliffs.

For eighteen grueling months, Julian’s fifteen-year-old daughter, Evelyn, had sat beside the grand arched windows of the cliffside room. Once an athletic teenager who raced down the rocky timber trails, she was now immobilized in a custom leather reclining chair. Her legs remained motionless beneath thick woolen blankets, and her voice had vanished completely into an unyielding, protective silence.
