The Provenance Ledger

Falsely accused of theft as a young apprentice, archivist Clara Mercer rises to become a lead heritage auditor. When she encounters her former tormentor at a high-society Newport gala, Clara does not seek revenge—she presents flawless empirical evidence that unravels a major corporate forgery.

Fictional story. Contains mild themes of past workplace/school social ostracization, corporate fraud, and mild verbal confrontation. No graphic violence, hate speech, or illegal instructions.

Chapter 1: The Gilded Gallery

The Grand Ballroom of Marble Crest Manor smelled of white lilies, expensive wax polish, and the cold salt air drifting off Narragansett Bay. Crystal chandeliers hung from vaulted ceilings, casting warm fractures of light across polished mahogany floors. For most of the evening's patrons, the annual Newport Heritage Gala was a celebration of legacy, lineage, and quiet wealth. For Clara Mercer, standing near the archway in a simple navy tailored suit, it was a room full of ghosts that had lost their power to haunt.

Twelve years ago, Clara had walked through these same carved doors as an unpaid conservation intern. Her father had worked as a night electrician for the local utility company, while her mother managed a dry-cleaning shop three miles inland. Clara had earned her place at Marble Crest through sheer academic discipline, spending her weekends restoring water-damaged manuscripts in the basement archives. She had believed, with the naive clarity of youth, that meticulous skill would earn her a seat among the city's celebrated preservationists.
