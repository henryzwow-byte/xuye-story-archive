The Barometer Ledger

When a woodworker receives a heavy antique brass barometer from her estranged brother, she discovers stamped copper plates hidden inside its casing—exposing a dangerous industrial scandal that brings ruthless operatives to her door.

Fictional story. Contains themes of family estrangement, corporate harassment, mild suspense, and industrial safety violations.

Chapter 1: The Crate from Penobscot

The freight haul truck pulled up outside Hannah Vance’s woodworking shop at ten in the morning, its diesel exhaust mixing with the damp Atlantic fog drifting off Penobscot Bay. Hannah had just finished wiping linseed oil off a restored oak mantle when the driver hauled a heavy, timber-slatted crate onto her loading dock.

"Freight charges due on receipt," the driver said, tapping a digital clipboard against his rain-slicked jacket. "Shipped from a marine salvage yard down in Portland, flagged as priority hold."
