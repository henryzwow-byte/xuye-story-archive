The Cape Alder Covenant

When a corporate syndicate threatens to condemn an elderly widow's coastal slipway, a struggling marine welder steps into freezing surf to save her dock. His selfless act uncovers a legacy covenant that reshapes both their lives.

Fictional story. Depicts financial stress, legal disputes, and mild peril during a stormy rescue effort. Contains no real-world legal or technical advice.

Chapter 1: The Salt on the Glass

The salt on the window pane had dried into gray, fan-shaped crystals that blurred the mouth of Cape Alder harbor. Out past the breaker rocks, the North Pacific was turning the color of wet slate under a heavy November sky. Inside the corrugated-iron shed of Vance Diesel & Marine Repair, the air smelled of diesel exhaust, scorched iron, and old sea boots.

Harlan Vance was forty-two, though his hands looked a decade older. His knuckles were permanently stained with crankcase grease, and his shoulders carried the quiet stoop of a man who spent his days bent inside boat engine compartments. He stood over a workbench, filing the burred edges of an impeller shaft, listening to the metallic rattle of the iron roof whenever the sea wind surged.
