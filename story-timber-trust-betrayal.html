Timber Trust Betrayal

In a Oregon timber township, artisan woodworker Caleb Vance builds a sanctuary for his brain-injured sister, only to uncover that her refined private caregiver is secretly sedating her to steal their inherited timber estate.

Fictional story. Contains themes of vulnerable adult exploitation, financial fraud, medical mistrust, and legal resolution. All characters and situations are entirely fictional.

Chapter 1: The Sanctuary at Cedar Creek

The mountain breeze blowing across Cedar Creek, Oregon, always carried the sharp, comforting scent of red cedar and damp pine needles. Nestled against the tree-covered ridge sat the timber home and workshop of Caleb Vance, a forty-two-year-old master woodworker whose hand-carved dining tables and custom cabinetry were renowned across the Pacific Northwest. Two years ago, following a devastating highway collision that left his twenty-eight-year-old sister, Hannah, coping with a traumatic brain injury and severe physical limitations, Caleb had designed and built a sunlit, barrier-free annex suite attached to the main workshop. It was meant to be her sanctuary, a place where she could regain her strength away from impersonal rehabilitation wards.

Six months prior, Julian Hayes had arrived as an answer to Caleb's prayers. Recommended by a regional private health agency, Julian possessed impeccable credentials as a certified physical rehabilitation specialist. Crisp in his tailored linen shirts, articulate, and unfailingly composed, he brought an aura of professional competence that immediately eased Caleb's anxiety. Julian established meticulous exercise schedules, maintained color-coded therapy binders, prepared tailored nutrition plans, and spent long hours guiding Hannah through cognitive memory drills. For the first time since the accident, Caleb felt he could focus on his woodworking without the constant, gnawing dread of leaving his sister vulnerable.
