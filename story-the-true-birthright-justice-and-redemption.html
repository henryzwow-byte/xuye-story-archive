The True Birthright: Justice and Redemption

Evidence uncovered by Maya brings down the corrupt Vance empire; in the aftermath, justice restores stolen wealth and gives her a family, an education, and a mission of her own.

Fictional story. Contains arrest, corporate fraud, child endangerment, imprisonment, and references to exploitation.

Chapter 1: The Gathering of Vipers

Julian tried to bolt toward the kitchen service exit, but two heavy-set federal agents intercepted him instantly, slamming him against a polished marble pillar. The sharp, metallic click of steel handcuffs echoed through the silent, glittering ballroom, permanently shattering the illusion of their untouchable power.

"Get your hands off me! Do you know who I am?!" Eleanor screamed as an agent twisted her arms firmly behind her back, the cold steel constraints ruining the pristine fabric of her expensive silver gown. "This is a setup! I will buy this city and fire every single one of you!"

"You won't be buying anything, Eleanor," Judge Sterling said coldly, walking over to Maya and gently placing a protective, reassuring hand on the young girl's trembling shoulder. "The FBI has been monitoring your offshore transactions and corporate conspiracies for weeks. This brave young lady didn't just save my life tonight—she just handed us the missing piece of evidence required to lock you away for the rest of your natural life."

As the disgraced socialites were dragged out of the Plaza Hotel in front of the very elite peers they had tried so desperately to impress, the high-society crowd turned their faces away in absolute disgust, completely abandoning the ruined vipers to save their own reputations.
