The Timber and the Shadow

When an ambitious landscape architect secretly leaves her husband for a high-stakes retreat with a charismatic former flame, a forgotten cedar chest in their workshop unveils a past that threatens to shatter their marriage forever.

Fictional story. Contains themes of emotional dishonesty, romantic boundary violations, and marital conflict.

Chapter 1: The Smell of Sawdust and Cedar

The air in our workshop always smelled of cedar shavings, damp earth, and the mineral tang of old cast-iron tools. For five years, my husband, Marcus, had spent his evenings framing the heavy Douglas fir timber that supported our dream: turning a century-old coastal barn in Oregon into a community design studio and cider house. His hands were calloused, his flannel shirts permanently dusted with fine yellow timber grit, and his commitment to our joint venture was absolute.

I had spent those same five years convincing myself that I was equally committed. My name is Elena Sterling, I am thirty-two, and until three weeks ago, I believed that a stable marriage was built on shared goals and quiet routines.
