Revoked Guarantee

Hours after signing her divorce agreement, architectural conservator Claire Mercer revokes her former husband's family from her business escrow account. When they show up at her studio demanding funds with a forged addendum, a paper trail of truth secures her complete freedom.

Fictional story. Depicts personal separation, emotional conflict, financial dispute, and legal boundaries. All characters, businesses, and incidents are entirely fictional.

Chapter 1: The Quiet Ledger

The ink on the dissolution decree was barely dry when Julian called, his voice cutting sharp through the quiet of my new studio. Morning fog clung to the windows of the Astoria waterfront workshop, blurring the cold gray waters of the Columbia River. For years, I had lived with a knot of tension behind my ribs, waiting for the next domestic fire to extinguish. But as I set down my copper measuring rule beside the drafting table, I felt only a cool, unexpected stillness.

"What did you do to the heritage restoration line, Claire?" Julian demanded, skipping any attempt at greeting. Through the speaker, background traffic hummed from downtown Seattle. "Chloe was at the international timber exchange trying to lock down a shipment of quarter-sawn white oak for her boutique gallery, and the bank flagged her corporate card as unauthorized."
