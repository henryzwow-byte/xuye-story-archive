Broken Timber Covenant

After a devastating structural collapse leaves Clara Vance needing urgent spinal surgery, her father refuses help to fund her brother's polo syndicate. A sacrificial gesture from her young cousin unlocks a long-hidden estate fraud and changes their fortunes forever.

Fictional story. Contains depictions of family neglect, workplace injury recovery, and financial disputes. All characters and events are entirely fictional.

Chapter 1: The Fracture at Cedar Cove

The heavy stench of rubbing alcohol, raw cedar sawdust, and industrial floor wax hung thick in the examination room of the Coos Bay Orthopedic Center. Clara Vance sat gripping the vinyl edges of the exam bench, her jaw set tight against the burning nerve pain radiating down her lower back and right leg. Two days earlier, while restoring the framing of a mid-nineteenth-century timber barn along the Oregon coast, a seasoned Douglas fir beam had snapped its iron hoisting clamp. The sudden collapse shattered her scaffolding bracket and sent Clara falling twelve feet onto a granite sill.

The orthopedic surgeon had just pulled up the high-resolution MRI scans on his wall monitor. Pointing his pen at a severe L5-S1 lumbar disc herniation compressing the primary nerve root, his diagnosis was stark. If she did not undergo specialized microdiscectomy and spinal graft stabilization by Friday morning, the motor nerve damage could quickly become irreversible, leaving her with permanent foot drop and a lifelong limp. Clara was thirty-two, a highly skilled timber restorer who built her livelihood on physical strength, balance, and precision woodworking.
