FICTIONAL STORY · A-221

The Tide and the Timber

When Claire Sterling is ordered by her mother to manage coat check at her sister’s high-society engagement gala, she complies quietly. But when the groom’s grandfather recognizes the forensic auditor who saved his family empire from ruin, the family’s carefully curated hierarchy crumbles.

Fictional story. Contains depictions of family emotional manipulation, verbal condescension, and social rank conflict.

Chapter 1: The Brass Token

The Atlantic ocean roared softly against the granite cliffs of Newport, Rhode Island, sending a fine salty mist against the tall arched windows of the Cliff House estate. Inside the grand conservatory, warm yellow light reflected off polished mahogany floors, silver champagne buckets, and towering arrangements of white hydrangeas. The air smelled of ocean spray, fresh lilies, and the vintage perfume of two hundred wealthy guests gathered to celebrate the engagement of Genevieve Sterling to Marcus Vance, the junior director of Vance Maritime Logistics.

I was standing near the mahogany entryway when my mother, Victoria Sterling, approached me. She did not offer a kiss on the cheek or ask how my drive down from Providence had been. Instead, she adjusted the lapels of her silk jacket, surveyed my dark slate knee-length dress with a critical squint, and thrust a heavy wooden tray of brass coat-check tokens directly into my hands.
