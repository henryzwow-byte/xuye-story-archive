The Hollow Pendulum

When a retired harbor surveyor visits her coastal workshop during a violent storm, horologist Evelyn Vance unearths a sealed brass canister hidden beneath her floorboards—discovering that her husband's late-night work masks a historic deception.

Fictional story. Contains themes of spousal deception, betrayal, financial fraud, and suspenseful confrontation.

Chapter 1: The Stranger at the Coastal Door

"Do not let him move the survey tripod tonight, even if he swears the county requested it."

Those were the words left behind by the wet stranger who stood on my doorstep while the Pacific gale hammered against the cedar shingles of our home in Cove Creek, Oregon. He did not ask for money or shelter inside the house. He only stood in the sea spray, his oilskin coat heavy with moisture, resting a trembling hand against the wooden railing of my workshop porch. In his palm, he carried a small, tarnished brass calibration key stamped with the emblem of the old timber recorder’s office—a seal that had not been used in our county for over thirty years.
