FICTIONAL STORY · A-393

The Sealed Compass

When Laurel is banished to a drafty timber shed during the grand opening of the maritime heritage center she spent three years restoring, a hidden codicil from her grandfather exposes years of family manipulation.

Fictional story. Depicts family emotional manipulation, financial exploitation, and non-violent interpersonal conflict.

Chapter 1: The Shed Behind the Yard

The brass lanterns of the Blackwood Maritime Pavilion cast a golden shimmer across the harbor, illuminating the glass doors where two hundred invited guests in tailored tuxedos and evening gowns gathered. Inside, champagne flutes clinked beneath polished oak rafters, celebrating the grand reopening of the historic nineteenth-century shipyard. Outside, the autumn night wind smelled of salt, damp cedar, and diesel exhaust from the temporary generator running behind the lumber shed. I stood on the gravel path holding a heavy cream seating card, staring at the crisp black copperplate lettering pressed into the paper: Laurel Vance — Outbuilding 3.

For a fleeting moment, I honestly believed it had to be a clerical error. Outbuilding 3 was a drafty timber storage shed sixty yards behind the main pavilion, past stacks of weathered spruce beams and rusted anchor chains. Inside, a single folding plastic chair sat beside a wooden worktable covered in sawdust, grease, and discarded staging rags. A lone incandescent bulb hung from a wooden rafter overhead, buzzing faintly against the coastal dampness.
