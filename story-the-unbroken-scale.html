The Unbroken Scale

In the high desert of Oregon, an independent scale house owner builds an unassailable digital telemetry trap to dismantle a corrupt transport auditor's reign of extortion.

Fictional story. Contains mild themes of coercion, extortion, and non-graphic physical intimidation.

Chapter 1: The High Desert Toll

The wind coming off the Columbia Plateau always carried the smell of dry chaff, diesel smoke, and cold grease. At five o'clock on an October dusk, the sodium lights along Highway 97 hummed into life, casting long amber shadows across the concrete deck of Valley Freight & Scale. Jesse Vance stood inside the glass-fronted scale booth, his fingers resting on the smooth brass casing of the digital weight indicator. For nine years, ever since his father’s heart had given out behind that same counter, Jesse had kept the deck calibrated to within two pounds of absolute zero. In a county where grain haulers, cattle truckers, and timber drivers lived by the margin between gross weight and payload, Jesse’s scale was the only thing on the corridor that didn't lie.

Except for Inspector Eli Thorne.
