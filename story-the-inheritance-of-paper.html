The Inheritance of Paper

After her family dumps her belongings into a rainstorm, a forensic accountant discovers a hidden trust and the paper trail someone tried to bury.

Fictional story. Contains emotional abuse, family estrangement, property destruction, inheritance fraud allegations, and legal disputes.

Chapter 1: The Mud and the Mailbox

The rain was hard enough to blur the old farmhouse before Adele Fairbanks understood that the dark shapes scattered across the driveway were her belongings.

A college book lay open in the mud. Her winter coat twisted across the grass. A shoebox of childhood letters had collapsed beneath the water.
