FICTIONAL STORY · A-378

The Silent Gear

When an arrogant business partner tries to steal a historic astronomical clock and oust a faithful archivist, master horologist Julian Mercer reveals that his steady hands—and his sharp intellect—never left.

Fictional story. Contains themes of workplace deception, corporate fraud, and emotional manipulation.

Chapter 1: The Dust on the Gears

The brass pendulum of the grand regulator clock in the foyer of Mercer & Croft Horology beat with a heavy, steady cadence, but inside the glass workshop, silence felt far more precarious. Rain streaked the tall mullioned windows overlooking Boston’s Beacon Hill, casting gray shadows across mahogany cabinets filled with two centuries of precision tools. For eighteen months, those tools had sat untouched by their owner. Julian Mercer sat behind a dark oak desk in the corner, his right wrist wrapped in a dark silk sleeve, his fingers resting motionless against a leather blotter. To the clients who frequented the atelier, he was a ghost of master craftsmanship—a man whose legendary hands had been ruined in a workshop explosion, leaving him a quiet spectator to his own name.

Across the polished parquet floor, Harrison Croft stood surrounded by three prospective investors in tailored Italian topcoats. Harrison was thirty-eight, sharp-featured, and dressed with the ostentatious perfection of a man who believed arrogance was merely a brand strategy. He gestured with a gold pen toward the center of the showroom, where a tall velvet-draped pedestal stood under soft halogen spotlights. Beneath the velvet rested the 1792 St. Jude Astronomical Clock, a historical masterpiece commissioned by a French duke and valued at eight million dollars.
