FICTIONAL STORY · A-405

Ironwood Tides

Gathering at a coastal Maine maritime center for her great-aunt's birthday, Tessa Vance faces condescension from wealthy relatives determined to sell the historic slipway. But her quiet archival work conceals a far greater stake in the land than they ever imagined.

Fictional story. Contains mild themes of family conflict and financial friction.

Chapter 1: The Salt on the Wind

The salt fog rolled heavy across Blackwood Cove as Tessa Vance pulled her faded green Ford pickup into the gravel lot of the Ironwood Maritime Center. Moisture hung thick in the air, clinging to the cedar shingles of the old boat slips and turning the harbor into a sheet of muted pewter. Tessa sat behind the wheel for a quiet moment, feeling the steady thrum of the engine fade away. She had spent the four-hour drive up the Maine coastline reminding herself of a simple promise: she was here to celebrate her great-aunt Marion’s eightieth birthday, not to reignite old friction with relatives who measured human worth strictly by balance sheets.

That promise began to fray the moment she stepped onto the damp gravel. Standing near the glass entrance of the restored timber hall were her uncle Julian and her cousin Derek, posed beside a freshly washed luxury SUV. Julian wore a tailored navy blazer over a crisp linen shirt, his hair silvered and swept back with deliberate care. Derek leaned against the tailgate, checking a leather-bound tablet, looking every bit the commercial real estate broker who believed historic harbors existed only to be subdivided into luxury condos.
