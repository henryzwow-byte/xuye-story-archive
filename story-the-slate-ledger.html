The Slate Ledger

When historic architectural restorer Clara Thorne discovers a corporate plot to cover up catastrophic structural flaws at a high-society dedication gala, she risks her career to present indisputable physical evidence of corruption.

Fictional story. Contains themes of workplace deception, corporate corruption, and professional vindication.

Chapter 1: The Defaced Vault

The smell hit me before the dust settled inside the lower archive of Sentinel Hall. It wasn't the honest odor of aged pine, cured lime, or damp granite that usually filled the historic waterfront customs house. It was the sharp, synthetic tang of commercial solvent, bitter enough to burn the back of my throat.

On the heavy oak worktable beneath the bare brass lamp, the master restoration journal lay open. Its thick parchment pages—hand-stitched fifty years ago by my late mentor, Eli Vance—were warped and translucent. Someone had poured industrial varnish stripper straight across the structural notes, smearing Eli’s iron-gall ink into useless charcoal clouds.
