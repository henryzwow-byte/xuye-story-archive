The Keeper of the Glass

When her step-sister publicizes her modest bequest of old drafting tools and glass negatives, conservator Clara Thorne uncovers a hidden stewardship trust that changes everything.

Fictional story. Depicts corporate betrayal, probate disputes, and workplace hostility in a professional environment.

Chapter 1: The Varnish Stain

The dark amber coat of historic iron gall varnish splashed across Clara Thorne’s linen shirt before she saw her step-sister’s hand move. It spread rapidly across the woven fabric, dark and heavy under the gallery spotlights, smelling sharply of boiled linseed, turpentine, and old resin.

Victoria Vanderford stepped back with polished grace, holding the empty crystal beaker by its stem as if setting down a prop after a finished performance. “Oh, darling, how clumsy of me,” Victoria said, her smile impeccably painted and entirely cold. “Though I suppose dark amber suits a custodian better than pale silk. Marcus always said you were happiest covered in sawdust and oil anyway.”
