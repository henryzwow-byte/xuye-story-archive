Cracks in the Seawall

Recovering from surgical delivery, structural engineer Clara Vance discovers her husband forged her professional seal on a compromised coastal foundation. Gathering digital telemetry and audit trails, she acts to protect her infant son and public safety.

Fictional story. Contains depictions of emotional neglect, marital conflict, and legal disputes.

Chapter 1: The Salt and the Silence

Three weeks after an emergency surgical delivery, the air in the oceanfront dining room of Crestwood Cove smelled of damp cedar, sea fog, and antiseptic salve. Clara Vance sat in the high-backed rocking chair, her feet swollen and her abdominal stitches throbbing with a dull, rhythmic ache every time she shifted her posture. On her chest lay her newborn son, Jonah, whose small fingers clutched at the collar of her worn flannel shirt. He had been crying fitfully for hours, distressed by the raw chill that seeped through the unsealed window frames of the old timber estate.

Across the mahogany table, her husband, Ethan Vance, leaned over a set of architectural blueprints for the Cliffside Marina Resort—a multi-million-dollar development perched along the unstable granite bluffs of the bay. Sitting beside him was his older sister, Corinne, who served as the firm’s chief financial administrator. Corinne tapped her acrylic nails against a crystal water glass, the steady, metallic click sounding like a metronome marking Clara's perceived inadequacy. Neither of them had offered to fetch Clara a fresh heating pad or hold Jonah so she could sit upright.
