Tides of Deception

A retired surveyor races to his daughter's home after his young granddaughter whispers of an emergency. What begins as a domestic confrontation reveals a calculated plot to frame an innocent inspector for illegal maritime dumping.

Fictional story. Depicts family tension, psychological deceit, and allegations of illegal environmental dumping. Contains no graphic violence.

Chapter 1: The Static on the Line

When my ten-year-old granddaughter Sophie called my workshop, the first sound I heard was not a voice, but the wet thrum of rain beating against the corrugated tin of her family's garden shed.

She was crouching low, breathing softly into the receiver as though the air itself might betray her location.
