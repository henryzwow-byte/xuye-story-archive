The Stone Ledger

After her family liquidates her home and workshop during her son's medical crisis, Hannah rebuilds her life as a county compliance officer—until her father comes to her desk seeking an emergency loan extension.

Fictional story. Contains themes of family estrangement, financial conflict, and child medical emergency.

Chapter 1: The Cold Corridor

The pediatric emergency wing of Bozeman Regional Hospital carried a bitter draft that smelled of floor wax, wet wool, and cheap espresso. Outside the tall frosted windows, a late November gale screamed across the Gallatin Valley, driving hard snow against the glass in rhythmic, sweeping bursts.

Hannah Reynolds sat on the edge of a vinyl bench, her work boots still dusted with frozen mud. Through the narrow observation panel of Room 204, she could see her eleven-year-old son, Leo. He lay motionless beneath a heavy white thermal blanket, his right arm immobilized in a rigid fiberglass cast and a pulse-oximeter clip glowing crimson on his index finger.
