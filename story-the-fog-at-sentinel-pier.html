The Fog at Sentinel Pier

When a midnight call reveals his young daughter stranded in freezing fog outside a locked boathouse, a bridge inspector uncovers a bitter plot to exploit his family—and fights to secure her future.

Fictional story. Contains mild themes of familial distress, emotional manipulation, and child endangerment.

Chapter 1: The Midnight Call

The heavy rain along Oregon's coastal corridors was turning the asphalt slick and reflective when Daniel Vance’s phone chimed on the motel nightstand. It was 1:12 a.m. Daniel, a veteran structural bridge inspector, had spent the evening reviewing high-resolution acoustic scans of concrete footings near Eugene. He reached for the phone expecting a routine update from his field crew, but the screen displayed a name he had not seen in months: Hannah Albright.

Hannah was a sixty-two-year-old retired harbor dispatcher who managed the small boatyard adjoining Daniel’s late grandfather’s property in Sentinel Point. She was a practical, soft-spoken woman who checked her crab pots at dawn and never called anyone after dark unless a mooring line had snapped in a gale.
