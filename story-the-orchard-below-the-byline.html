The Orchard Below the Byline

After twelve years saving her family's Hudson Valley orchard from foreclosure, Nora finds a luxury magazine crediting the estate to her sister and her sister's fiancé. The land records, financing files, and permits tell a different story—and force Nora to stop confusing rescue with love.

Fictional story. Contains themes of familial financial exploitation, emotional alienation, and legal disputes.

Chapter 1: The Glossy Erasure

The morning mist hung low over the Hudson Valley, wrapping the red oak rafters of the workshop in damp, sweet-smelling air. Nora Hayes stood by the stainless-steel sink with a chipped stoneware mug in her hand, watching rain trickle down the glass. Her hands were calloused from twelve years of real work—pruning apple boughs, sealing cider casks, and scraping moss off slate roofs. Everything about her morning felt predictable until her laptop screen chimed with an automated alert from Architectural Haven.

The headline was set in elegant serif typography: “Restoring the Ancestral Soil: How Tess Hayes Built the Valley’s Most Exclusive Eco-Resort.”
