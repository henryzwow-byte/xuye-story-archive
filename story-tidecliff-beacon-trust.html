The Beacon at Tidecliff

When predatory family members corner a recovering conservator in her cliffside workshop to seize a historic trust, a hidden archival recording system exposes their conspiracy.

Fictional story. Contains themes of family betrayal, physical coercion, and emotional distress.

Chapter 1: The Sanctuary at Tidecliff

The thick Pacific fog rolled over the jagged cliffs of Tidecliff Bay, wrapping the coastal pines in a quiet, slate-gray blanket. Inside her timber-framed workshop adjoining the cottage, Clara Linwood sat at an oak drafting table, the soft glow of a brass lamp illuminating her work. Her left leg was encased in a rigid post-surgical knee brace, propped carefully on a leather footstool. Three weeks had passed since her reconstructive joint surgery, and while her physical therapist promised a full recovery, every movement still required deliberate care and aluminum crutches.

Clara focused her attention on a century-old stained-glass panel from the Tidecliff Light Station lantern room. As an archival conservator, she possessed a steady hand and a deep reverence for local history. The light station had been her late father’s life work during his forty years as harbor master. Before his passing, he had established the Linwood Historic Conservation Trust—a protected $180,000 community endowment dedicated exclusively to preserving the town’s 19th-century maritime lighthouse and coastal archives. Clara served as the primary trustee, a role she took seriously not just as a professional duty, but as a sacred family promise.
