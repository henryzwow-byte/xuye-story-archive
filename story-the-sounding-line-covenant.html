The Sounding Line Covenant

Following the oceanographic loss of her engineer husband, Mara Thorne faces a hostile custody fight and forced eviction by her late husband's powerful family. But a sealed international covenant and a secret bond acquisition turn her home into an impenetrable fortress.

Fictional story. Contains themes of familial grief, bereavement, high-stakes corporate probate disputes, and legal confrontation. All characters and organizations are entirely fictitious.

Chapter 1: The Tide at Blackwood Cove

The rain off the Pacific Northwest coast did not fall so much as cling, wrapping the weathered cedar beams of the Blackwood Bay boathouse in a damp, silver shroud. Inside the high-ceilinged timber workshop, the rich scent of fresh teak wood shavings, boiled linseed oil, and cold sea salt hung heavy in the air. Mara Thorne stood beside the heavy oak workbench, her hands lightly dusted with dried varnish, watching her five-year-old daughter Iris trace small, deliberate circles on a slab of polished driftwood with a piece of white chalk.

Four months had passed since the oceanographic research vessel Aegir vanished during an offshore acoustic canyon survey, taking Mara's husband, Julian Vance, into the silent depths of the Pacific. Julian had been a brilliant hydro-acoustic engineer—a quiet man who listened to sub-oceanic soundscapes with a fierce, solitary devotion. In the painful aftermath of his loss, Mara had withdrawn from the world, seeking sanctuary in the coastal restoration shop they had built together on this rocky Oregon bluff. Here, far removed from the towering glass skyscrapers and high-stakes boardrooms of San Francisco, Mara believed she had constructed a boundary strong enough to protect their fragile new reality.
