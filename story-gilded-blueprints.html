The Gilded Blueprints

When structural engineer Teresa Vance is invited to her ex-husband's glamorous harbor gala and seated near the service kitchen, she attends with her five-year-old twin daughters and a portfolio that rewrites the family's legacy.

Fictional story. Contains themes of high-society conflict, marital separation, and personal resilience.

Chapter 1: The Golden Crest

The gilded card arrived on a rain-slicked Thursday in Port Townsend, sandwiched between a marine safety newsletter and a preschool art supply catalog. It was heavy, heavy enough to weigh down the corner of the wooden kitchen counter, pressed with the embossed crest of Mercer Logistics & Maritime.

For a long moment, the only sound in the seaside cottage was the rhythmic hum of the dehumidifier in the hallway and the clatter of wooden building blocks in the living room. Five-year-old Maeve was constructing a miniature suspension bridge while her twin sister, Clara, carefully balanced toy tugboats beneath the spans.
