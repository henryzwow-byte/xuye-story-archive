The Glassmaker's Covenant

When stained-glass conservator Clara Vance is publicly humiliated by her son and daughter-in-law at a luxury estate unveiling, she quietly triggers the protective clauses of her family guild charter, uncovering financial misconduct and reclaiming control of her life's work.

Fictional story. Contains themes of familial conflict, emotional abuse, betrayal, and financial deception.

Chapter 1: The Shattered Medallion

The heavy timber doors of Seacliff Manor swung open, releasing a wave of warm air scented with imported cedar, roasted figs, and expensive champagne. Outside, the autumn rain slashed against the cliffs of the Oregon coast, soaking the hem of Clara Vance’s heavy woolen coat. She stood in the marble vestibule, sixty-four years old, holding a padded leather satchel against her chest like a relic. Inside the satchel lay an amber glass medallion—a restorer’s masterpiece featuring etched pine needles and hand-spun lead came, crafted over four weeks in her coastal workshop to serve as the centerpiece for the manor’s restored conservatory dome.

Clara wiped a bead of rainwater from her forehead with a thumb calloused by forty years of handling glass cutters and soldering irons. She had imagined this evening with quiet anticipation. She pictured her son, Arthur, stepping away from his wealthy investors, offering a proud smile, and introducing her to the grand opening crowd as the master conservator whose hands had built the foundation of Vance Heritage Studios.
