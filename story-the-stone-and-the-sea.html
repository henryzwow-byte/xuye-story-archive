The Stone and the Sea

On a wind-bitten cliff in coastal Oregon, Clara Vance faces the calculated cruelty of her husband's mother. Locked out in a storm while seven months pregnant, her quiet resolve and an unshakeable truth dismantle a family empire's illusions.

Fictional story. Contains depictions of family hostility, emotional abuse, harsh weather exposure, and pregnancy stress.

Chapter 1: The Precipice of Highcliff

The gale howling off the Pacific did not care about the history of Highcliff Manor. It slammed against the granite balustrades, throwing ocean spray and freezing rain across the wide terrace. Standing in the darkness outside the heavy oak doors, Clara Bennett Vance pressed both hands against her rounded stomach, keeping her back pinned against the stone wall where the wind could not reach her directly.

The fabric of her emerald wool coat was drenched at the shoulders, heavy and icy against her skin. Underneath, her baby kicked—a firm, steady movement that felt like a quiet promise in the storm. Clara leaned her head back against the cold masonry, drawing a deep, slow breath into her lungs. "We are okay," she whispered into the rush of rain. "You and I are entirely fine."
