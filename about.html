About Story Archive

Story Archive is a public, mobile-friendly library of complete fictional stories.

What this site does

The archive turns manuscripts supplied for publication into searchable, shareable story pages. Every story has a stable public link and can be read without registration.

Publisher and editorial responsibility

Story Archive Editorial Desk is the independent publisher and maintainer of this archive. It selects, edits, translates, formats and presents fictional manuscripts for public reading. Story Archive is not a news organization, and its stories must not be understood as factual reporting.

Language editions

English and Simplified Chinese are editorial editions. The language menu can provide automatic translations of the public site for readers in other regions.

Copyright and adaptations

The archive may publish original fiction and editorial adaptations prepared from manuscripts supplied by an author, rights holder or another person with permission to publish. Credible rights concerns are reviewed, and affected material may be corrected or removed.
