The Owl of Juniper Ridge

When a solar pump mechanic stops to rescue a trapped Great Horned Owl along a dusty orchard road, a strange brass token entangled in its talons uncovers an urgent rescue mission in the high mountain canyons.

Fictional story. Contains mild themes of wilderness rescue and animal injury.

Chapter 1: The Dust on Pine Creek Road

Marcus Finch killed the ignition of his Ford F-250 and sat in the quiet heat of the cab, letting the engine ticking cool down in the late-afternoon light. Dust settled over the cracked windshield like fine cinnamon. He had spent eleven straight hours crawling through gravel pits and sun-baked pump houses along the lower canal, rebuilding a seized three-phase impeller for the valley irrigation district. His shoulders ached with the deep, hollow strain that came from hauling ninety-pound electric motors across dry ditch banks, and his fingernails were stained with black lithium grease that no shop soap in Yakima County could completely lift.

He reached into his breast pocket and tapped the screen of his phone. It was 5:12 p.m.
