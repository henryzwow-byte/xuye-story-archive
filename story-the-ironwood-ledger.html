The Ironwood Ledger

When her husband abandons her in a leaking historical annex to entertain wealthy investors, archivist Evelyn Thorne invokes a hidden property trust that strips his firm of all authority.

Fictional story. Contains themes of emotional neglect, marital breach of trust, and financial disputes.

Chapter 1: The Cold Annex

The gale had been battering the slate roof of the Ironwood Maritime Archive since noon, but inside the cold stone annex, the air was thick with the smell of wet mortar, aged pine, and damp eighteenth-century paper. I stood on the granite floor with a thick linen cloth pressed against a seam in the leaking skylight, trying desperately to shield a stack of rare nautical charts from the encroaching storm. Water was already dripping steadily down the mortar wall, pooling near the legs of the heavy mahogany storage chests.

Across the courtyard, the main pavilion burned with warm, golden light. Champagne flutes clinked behind high arched windows, low laughter echoed off polished cedar beams, and prospective venture partners admired the historic coastal property I had spent three tedious years restoring. Tonight was supposed to be the inaugural preview of the archive, a celebration of coastal heritage that I had poured my heart into.
