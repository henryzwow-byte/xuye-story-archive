Fractured Bedrock

Suspended high above a storm-swollen gorge, structural inspector Nora Vance faces forced termination and public humiliation by a ruthless director. Armed with mechanical skill and hard data, she uncovers corporate sabotage while confronting five years of estrangement from her legendary father.

Fictional story. Contains depictions of professional hostility, corporate negligence, high-altitude industrial danger, and strained familial relationships.

Chapter 1: The Cold Gauge

Freezing rain misted off the sheer concrete monolith of the Diablo Gorge Hydroelectric Dam, carrying the bitter, metallic scent of churning mountain runoff. Forty meters above the white-water spillway basin, the wind shrieked through the open-lattice steel of the lower inspection gantry.

Nora Vance stood on the slick expanded-metal grating, her work boots locked in a stable stance despite the vibrations shivering through the superstructure. She wore a high-visibility harness weighted with diagnostic transducers, her oil-stained canvas jacket soaked through at the shoulders. Rain droplets clung to the dark stray hairs pulled loose from her hardhat harness.
