FICTIONAL STORY · A-431

The Harbor Covenant

Three weeks after her wedding, artisan glassblower Nora Vance discovers her mother-in-law invading her seaside workshop with a secret key fob. When a confrontation leads to spilled hot wax and a shattered commission, Nora relies on premarital deeds and documented evidence to reclaim her home and her freedom.

Fictional story. Contains depictions of family conflict, boundary violations, minor burn injury, and marital separation.

Chapter 1: The Glass and the Key

High on the granite bluffs of Cape Mist, Massachusetts, the Atlantic air always carried a sharp blend of salt spray and seasoned pine. Inside the historic brick-and-timber workshop overlooking the harbor, Nora Vance stood at her heavy maple worktable beneath soaring arched rafters. Her hands were precise and steady as she calibrated the hand-cut brass gears of a tide-chronometer. Around her stood rows of stained-glass lanterns, rolls of copper foil, and the rhythmic, comforting tick of hanging pendulum clocks.

She had been married to Julian Thorne for exactly twenty-two days. It was not nearly enough time for a shared life to find its rhythm or for small frictions to settle into predictable patterns. Unopened moving boxes filled with Julian’s architectural sketchbooks still lined the hallway of the residential loft above the workshop, and their formal wedding portraits remained unhung, leaning face-down against the brick fireplace downstairs.
