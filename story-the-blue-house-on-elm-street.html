The Blue House on Elm Street

A serviceman returns home to find his mother gone, her blue house under attack, and a forged deed pointing to a wider property scheme.

Fictional story. Contains bereavement, property destruction, physical confrontation, firearm threats, gunfire, fraud allegations, and criminal proceedings.

Chapter 1: The Broken Window

The first thing Elias Vance heard when he returned to Elm Street was glass breaking.

After three years away, he wanted only his mother’s coffee and the porch rail his father had painted. Instead, strangers were tearing apart her blue house.
