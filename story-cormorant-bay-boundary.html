Cormorant Bay Boundary

When an estranged relative leaves an intimidating document inside an eight-year-old child's instrument case, a mother and father must confront long-standing emotional manipulation and enforce strict boundaries to keep their family safe.

Fictional story. Depicts family emotional conflict, subtle psychological manipulation, and boundary enforcement. No graphic violence or legal advice.

Chapter 1: The Distant Bell

The heavy desk phone rang at 10:15 on a damp Tuesday morning in late August.

The sound cut through the salt-scented draft drifting from the bay, the rustle of vellum paper, and the rhythmic clicking of Claire Sterling’s drawing pen at the Cormorant Bay Maritime Archives. She was sketching a botanical plate of coastal kelp specimens for an upcoming museum publication, her right hand stained with India ink and her coffee half-empty beside a stack of reference folios.
