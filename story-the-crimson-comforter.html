The Crimson Comforter: Why an Elite Virginia Matriarch Desperately Concealed a Motionless Body, and the Terrifying Secret That Shattered the Vanguard Dynasty

Eleanor hides a drugged whistleblower to secure stolen drone technology, but her daughter and military husband expose the scheme before the entire Vanguard legacy is destroyed.

Fictional story. Contains unlawful confinement, drugging, corporate espionage, a slap, federal investigation, organized corruption, and imprisonment.

Chapter 1: The Threshold of Hysteria

The master suite of the Great Falls estate was thick with the scent of lavender and rising panic. Eleanor Vanguard was frantic, trying to arrange the pillows, her hands shaking violently against the rich fabric of the crimson comforter.

She had traded her husband's business partner's life—and silence—to a foreign corporate entity in exchange for the proprietary drone technology that would guarantee the Vanguard Group's dominance for the next half-century. She thought she could keep him in a medically induced twilight behind locked doors until the cross-border merger officially closed.

Every ticking second felt like a countdown to total ruin. But amid her frantic adjustments, she failed to notice the heavy, measured footsteps approaching from the hallway.
