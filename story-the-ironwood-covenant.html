FICTIONAL STORY · A-435

The Ironwood Covenant

A master timber craftsman in Idaho stands his ground when his son secretly maneuvers to convert their ancestral mountain forest into a luxury alpine resort.

Fictional story. Contains themes of family estrangement, financial deceit, corporate manipulation, and emotional conflict.

Chapter 1: The Unmarked Groundbreaking

Garrett Vance stood beside the cast-iron stove in his timber framing workshop, wiping cedar dust from the edge of a drafting table. Sunlight slanted through the high clerestory windows of the mountain shop, illuminating shavings of yellow pine scattered across the floor. For thirty-four years, the workshop had smelled of resin, damp stone, and linseed oil—the lingering essence of a lifetime spent shaping timber in the Sawtooth Valley of Idaho. It was a crisp Thursday morning, and Garrett had spent the early hours preparing the oak joinery for a timber bench he planned to build with his son, Julian.

They had agreed weeks ago to spend the weekend auditing the timber stock on Ironwood Crest, the four-hundred-acre ridge of red fir and ponderosa pine that had belonged to the Vance family for three generations. Garrett had stoked the workshop stove early, set out two tin mugs, and brewed a fresh pot of chicory coffee. He anticipated Julian’s truck arriving by ten, carrying the familiar clutter of surveying tools and blueprints that usually accompanied their shared projects.
